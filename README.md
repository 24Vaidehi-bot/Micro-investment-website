1) Project Name: Micro‑Invest – Investing Made Simple

2) Team Name: TriBots

3) Problem Statement : PS 13: INVESTING FOR EVERYONE – A MICRO-INVESTMENT PLATFORM FOR FIRST-TIME USERS A significant portion of India’s population—especially individuals from rural, semi-urban, and low-income communities—remains excluded from formal investing. Common barriers include: • Fear that investing is risky or meant only for the wealthy • Lack of financial literacy and difficulty understanding jargon • Distrust in existing financial applications • Limited access to high-end smartphones or stable internet • Perception that investing requires large amounts of money

Project Overview: ₹10 Se Shuru

Micro Invest is a micro-investment platform designed to make investing accessible, simple, and trustworthy for first-time and low-income users. The core idea is to allow people to start investing with as little as ₹10, breaking the psychological and financial barriers that prevent large sections of society from participating in wealth creation. The project correctly identifies real and widespread problems in India’s financial ecosystem:

1)Investing is perceived as risky, complex, and meant only for the rich

2)Low financial literacy, especially in rural and semi-urban areas

3)Existing platforms are complex, untrusted, and require high minimum investments

4)As a result, money remains idle as cash, leading to:

-No wealth creation

-Long-term financial insecurity

-Dependence on informal savings methods

Investing for Everyone
A micro-investment platform for first-time users. Minimum investment: ₹10 Focus on safe, low-risk options.
